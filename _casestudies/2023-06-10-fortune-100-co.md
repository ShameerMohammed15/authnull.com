## Introduction:
In an era of increasing cyber threats, organizations face the challenge of securing privileged access to their server infrastructure while simplifying user authentication. This case study showcases a Fortune 100 company that successfully implemented AuthNull, a cutting-edge privileged access management solution. By replacing Cyberark with AuthNull, the organization achieved significant improvements in security, operational efficiency, and cost savings.
<br/><br/>

### Background:
Our client, a Fortune 100 company, recognized the need to enhance their privileged access management capabilities. The existing solution, Cyberark, relied on password and SSH key-based authentication, which posed significant security risks and operational challenges. Seeking a more secure and efficient approach, the company chose to implement AuthNull.

<br/><br/>

## Implementation:
The implementation of AuthNull involved the following steps:

<br/><br/>

### Assessment and Planning:
AuthNull's team collaborated closely with the client's IT security experts to assess their infrastructure and privileged access requirements. This detailed assessment ensured that the implementation aligned with the company's specific needs.
<br/><br/>

### Solution Design and Integration:
Based on the assessment, AuthNull designed a robust solution that provided secure privileged access to the server infrastructure. AuthNull's unique approach enabled authentication to Posix or LDAP accounts without the need for passwords or SSH keys. The solution seamlessly integrated with the company's existing systems and supported various protocols, including RDP, SSH, and VNC.
<br/><br/>

### Migration and User Adoption:
AuthNull's team facilitated a smooth migration process, assisting in the transition from Cyberark to AuthNull. They provided comprehensive training sessions to educate employees on the benefits and usage of the new solution. The user-friendly interface and simplified authentication process accelerated user adoption and minimized disruption.

<br/><br/>

## Benefits Gained:
The implementation of AuthNull resulted in substantial benefits for the Fortune 100 company:
<br/><br/>

### Enhanced Security:
By decentralizing the storage of passwords and eliminating the use of SSH keys, AuthNull significantly reduced the attack surface. This approach prevented credential theft attacks, safeguarding the organization's sensitive data and critical infrastructure.
<br/><br/>

### Operational Efficiency:
AuthNull's streamlined privileged access management process reduced complexity and administrative overhead. The removal of the vault, a major attack vector in the previous system, simplified administration and eliminated the need for manual password management. This resulted in improved operational efficiency and reduced IT support efforts.
<br/><br/>

### Cost Savings:
By choosing AuthNull over Cyberark, the company achieved substantial cost savings. The elimination of Cyberark's licensing fees, along with the reduction in IT support efforts related to password management, resulted in net savings of over $2 million in the past 12 months alone. These cost savings allowed the company to allocate resources to other critical business initiatives.
<br/><br/>

## Conclusion:
The Fortune 100 company successfully replaced Cyberark with AuthNull, a secure and efficient privileged access management solution. AuthNull's unique features, such as passwordless authentication and support for various protocols, enabled the organization to strengthen security, enhance operational efficiency, and realize significant cost savings. By embracing AuthNull, the company achieved a robust privileged access management infrastructure, ensuring secure access to their server infrastructure while minimizing risks and improving productivity.<br/><br/>
