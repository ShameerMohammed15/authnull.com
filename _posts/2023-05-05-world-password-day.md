## Introduction:
<br/>
On World Password Day, we reflect on the limitations of traditional passwords and the potential for a passwordless future. Passwords are vulnerable to breaches, user frustrations, and complex management. Going passwordless provides enhanced security, improved user experience, and simplified IT infrastructure management. Passwordless authentication methods like biometrics, hardware tokens, and cryptographic keys offer stronger protection against credential-based attacks, eliminate password-related frustrations, and streamline authentication processes. It reduces the risk of breaches, aligns with regulatory requirements, and simplifies IT administration.  
<br/>

AuthNull made a commitment to eliminate passwords and SSH Keys in 2022 and since then we have been tirelessly working to translate that vision into reality. With the help of Decentralized identity, a wallet, verified credentials and various other standards, AuthNull has enabled the passwordless vision in IT infrastructure, a reality today.

### SMS OTP is not secure
<br/>

<div class="bg-blue-50 max-w-none">
<i>
 "Security researchers have demonstrated the increasing success (read: lower cost in time and effort and higher success rates) of redirecting or intercepting SMS messages en masse….It's not just the vulnerability of someone stealing your phone, it's about the SMS that's sent to the user being read by a malicious actor without getting her or his grubby paws on your phone. Because of the risks, we are discouraging the use of SMS as an “out of band authenticator” — which is, essentially, a method for delivering a one-time use code for multi-factor authentication"</i>- NIST Cybersecurity blog 
</div>
<br/>
NIST does not recommend the use of SMS OTP as a second factor and has strongly recommended deprecating the approach.
<br/><br/>

### OTP through authenticator apps is high friction
While OTP through authenticator apps maybe more secure than SMS OTP, it adds to needless friction, and is absolutely unusable a lot of critical use cases.
