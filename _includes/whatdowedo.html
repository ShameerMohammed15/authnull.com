Passwordless Privileged Access Management

AuthNull enables Passwordless Access to critical infrastructure.



The background

Why is this a problem?

Traditional Privileged Access Management (PAM) is hard both due to the fact that it involves (a) high friction for users (b) Passwords and SSH Key - rotation and distribution is both a challenge and painful and (c) a centralized attack surface means an imminent compromise.

How does AuthNull solve this problem?

AuthNull simplifies server access through a simplified 1FA or 2FA passwordless solution. We decentralize credenetials using industry standards such as Verified Credentials and introduce two roots of trust (Organization and end user wallet), and protect all credentials using public key cryptography using FIPS Compliant Digital Signature Standard (DSS)

How does AuthNull work?

AuthNull enables you to securely access remote servers without the need for Passwords, SSH keys and other credentials that can lead to credential compromise attacks.
