Passwordless Privileged Access Management

AuthNull enables Passwordless Privileged Access Management with Identity Aware authentication.



The background

Why is this a problem?

Managing, remembering SSH Keys and passwords is neither easy nor safe. Vaults are great, until they get hacked of course. A Privileged Access Management that does not have passwords to remember sure sounds like an oxymoron but that's exactly what is required to keep sensitive servers and data safe.

What does AuthNull do?

AuthNull enables you to manage, secure and deliver Privileged Access to Critical infrastructure with a Decentralized Authentication solution.

What kind of authentication support does AuthNull have?

Protocols supported - SSH, LDAP, Telnet, VNC, and RDP. OS - Wide range if *NIX (Windows support coming soon). Authentication support - local privileged users, or active directory.
