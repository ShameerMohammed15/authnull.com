What do we do?

We primarily enable passwordless authentication for Legacy infrastructure i.e. Linux VM and baremetal machines.

Why is this a problem?

Credential management done through centralized solutions exposes customers to hacks thanks to a centrally available attack vector. We aim to solve this problem by eliminating centrally stored passwords.

Why only support Linux?

Linux accounts for 70+% of the world's operating system for production workloads in enterprise servers.

What authentication factors are supported

We support SMS OTP, Mobile App OTP (soon) and Decentralized Wallet based credentials.
