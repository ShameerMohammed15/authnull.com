How does AuthNull's M2M authentication work?

Service accounts authentication works the same way traditional OS authentication works. Except since AuthNull is also a privileged access MFA solution these authentication are routed for verification through AuthNull. What this means is that if clear policies are not created, AuthNull will decline the authentication even though users might have access to necessary claims.

What are the key differentiators of service account features of AuthNull?

AuthNull's service features enable Linux M2M authentication, without just reliance on passwords, secrets or SSH Keys. This means there could be more than one factor available.

Does the Service account depend on a Human wallet?

When authentication policies and claims are created, there is a dependency on the wallet. Once the claims are delegated then there's no dependency on admin wallets.
