A radically better approach to Privileged Access

Vaultless design


AuthNull helps enables you to store vaults in a decentralized wallet, owned by the user and managed by two roots of trust.

Decentralized Authenticator Wallet

AuthNull's Authenticator Wallet also serves as a credential storage for recieving all credentials - passwordless credentials, as well as SSH Keys and passwords.

Automated distribution and lookups

When enabled, SSH Keys and Passwords (as one of the factors) are automatically rotated and distributed to the right wallet users who currently have the privileges to the same.
