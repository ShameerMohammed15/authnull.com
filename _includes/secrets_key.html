A radically better approach to Secrets store

Secrets Management

The AuthNull Secrets solution helps you to store and manage secrets without a central store. Simplify securing non human identities for apps, devops pipelines and cloud workloads.

Decentralized

A fully decentralized platform means credentials are distributed to individual users' devices., and that you do not need to manage a vault. This removes a single point of failure and dramatically improves security.

Automated Password Rotations for Local and Active Directory Accounts

Local endpoint based users, as well as active directory accounts are directly managed by the secrets manager. Password rotations help deliver a secure enterprise policy.

One Click Remote Connections

Connect to your infrastructure with one click remote connections eliminating typing of passwords.

Enterprise Ready

SIEM integrations, extensive auditing and automated offboarding / onboarding of users make it enterprise ready.
