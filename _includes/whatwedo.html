Why did we build AuthNull?

  • 01.
    Because Privileged Access Management should be easy

    AuthNull was built as a response to our own (and our customers') frustrations to current generation privileged access solutions. They are bulky, expensive and are hard deploy. This friction is the precise reason why IT admins do not want to deploy PAM solutions.

  • 02.
    Because passwords and SSH keys should be a thing of the past.

    Because passwords and SSH keys are directly responsible for 50% of all credential theft. It is time we eliminated passwords from IT infra.

  • 03.
    Because privileged access platforms are needlessly expensive

    It should not cost you 3 full time engineers to maintain a PAM platform.

  • 04.
    Because getting to Zero Trust should be easier

    Zero Trust (ZT) is something that everyone wants to get to, yet it is impossible with traditional systems to achieve. AuthNull was built to solve this need.
