Platform Features

Our platform provides lightweight, accelerated rollout of passwordless PAM on your infrastructure infrastructure.

SAML2 SSO

Login with a SSO Provider to enable administrators as well as end users to manage PAM infrastructure.

Active Directory Authentication

Enable Active Directory LDAP Authentication on your critical infrastructure while eliminating passwords and SSH Keys

Authenticator App for Decentralized Credentials

Authnull provides a full blown customizable authenticator app that can be used for Decentralized credentials for your organization. AuthNull uses W3C Verified Credential Specification for its decentralized credentials.

Enable 1FA or 2FA Passwordless

Enable passwordless decentralized credentials as a 2nd factor. Or fully eliminate passwords to deliver it as the only factor of authentication.
