Secrets Management

Store and Manage Secrets but without a secrets server

AuthNull provides secrets management without the hassle of managing a secret server infrastructure.

How does this work?
All credentials are stored decentrally in the users wallet (as assigned) without requiring a secrets server. Wallets use a public-private key pair that belongs to the user. Credentials are signed by organizations and sent to user's wallet to be signed with their private key.
Public key cryptography and two roots of trust.
AuthNull uses PKI with two roots of trust - Organization's public / private key and the user's public/private key pair. When credentials are issued, they are signed by the administrator with the tenant's private keys. Wallet credential stored in a user's phone with AuthNull app are protected by users' biometrics and signed by the users private key during authentication.

Security

Better security throgh Decentralized Infrastructure

AuthNull leverages Decentralized Infrastructure to provide better Identity by design

How does this work?
Decentralized Identity leverages various standard that deliver a distributed system without a single major attack vector - a better security approach by design, shield your organizations from a major attack on a central authority. With Decentralized Identity and Credentials, organizations are always in control while distributing credentials which are stored and managed on users' wallets.
What is required?
Decentralized identity requires the depoyment of a wallet app on your smart phones. This wallet app contains all of the user assigned credentials together with the users' private key. Users sign their credentials during authentication after signing with their private keys.
